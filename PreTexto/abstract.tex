\begin{abstractutfpr}
This work aims to investigate the feasibility of distributing a proof-of-concept application based on the Notification-Oriented Paradigm (NOP) in a Kubernetes cluster. The research considers aspects related to software architecture, component decoupling, inter-service communication, and computational resource utilization in distributed environments. The theoretical foundation draws on the principles of NOP and the NOP Query method, developed for processing queries over continuous data streams. Based on this foundation, the study proposes investigating the containerization and distribution of components of a notification-oriented application using Docker and Kubernetes. The proposed investigation encompasses the mechanisms required for application deployment, the identification of potential architectural limitations, and the observation of its execution in a distributed environment. The study is expected to identify the technical conditions required for distribution, as well as challenges involving communication, state management, and component organization. The investigation seeks to contribute to understanding the possibilities and limitations of deploying NOP-based applications on Kubernetes-orchestrated infrastructure, providing a basis for subsequent performance and scalability evaluations.
\end{abstractutfpr}
